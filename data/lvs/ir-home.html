Investor Highlights

Global Industry Leadership

We are the global leader in convention-based Integrated Resort development and operation, providing a platform within our host communities from which to deliver economic growth and employment opportunities while maintaining the highest levels of integrity and ethical standards

Delivering Growth

We are uniquely positioned to bring our unmatched track record and powerful convention-based business model to the most promising Integrated Resort development opportunities around the world

Driving Shareholder Return

We are committed to driving long-term shareholder value by delivering earnings growth and returning excess capital to shareholders

Returning Capital to Shareholders

Since initiating our capital return program in 2012, we have returned over $35.5 billion of capital to shareholders through dividends and share repurchases through June 30th, 2026

2025 Annual Report

In Macao in 2025 we completed the execution of significant capital investment at The Londoner Macao, positioning the company for future growth. At Marina Bay Sands in Singapore, we delivered more than $2.9 billion in adjusted property EBITDA, a record for any property in the history of our company. In addition, we repurchased $2.25 billion of stock through our share repurchase program.
