MATERIAL MODIFICATION TO RIGHTS OF SECURITY HOLDERS. On July&#160;17, 2026, M&T Bank Corporation (&#8220;M&T&#8221;) filed a certificate of amendment (the &#8220;Certificate of Amendment&#8221;) with the New York State Department of State establishing the rights, preferences, privileges, qualifications, restrictions and limitations of a new series of its preferred stock designated as the Perpetual 6.625% Non-Cumulative Preferred Stock, Series L, par value $1.00 per share, liquidation preference $10,000 per share (the &#8220;Series L Preferred Stock&#8221;). The Certificate of Amendment was filed in connection with an Underwriting Agreement, dated July&#160;16, 2026 (the &#8220;Underwriting Agreement&#8221;), with BofA Securities, Inc., J.P. Morgan Securities LLC, Morgan Stanley&#160;& Co. LLC, M&T Securities, Inc., Raymond James&#160;& Associates, Inc., UBS Securities LLC, and Wells Fargo Securities, LLC as representatives of the several underwriters named in Schedule A thereto (the &#8220;Underwriters&#8221;), under which M&T agreed to sell to the Underwriters 24,000,000 depositary shares (the &#8220;Depositary Shares&#8221;) each representing a 1/400th interest in a share of the Series L Preferred Stock. The Series L Preferred Stock ranks senior to the common stock of M&T, equally with M&T&#8217;s outstanding Series F, H, I, J and K preferred stock, and at least equally with each other series of preferred stock M&T may issue (except for any senior capital stock that may be issued with the requisite consent of the holders of the Series L Preferred Stock and all parity stock), with respect to payments of dividends and distributions of assets upon liquidation, dissolution or winding up. Under the terms of the Series L Preferred Stock, the ability of M&T to pay dividends on, make distributions with respect to, or to redeem, purchase or acquire, or make a liquidation payment on its common stock or any preferred stock ranking on a parity with or junior to the Series L Preferred Stock, is subject to restrictions in the event that M&T does not declare dividends on the Series L Preferred Stock for the most recently completed dividend period, or, in the case of a liquidation payment, does not pay to holders of the Series L Preferred Stock the stated amount of $10,000 per share, plus any declared and unpaid dividends, without accumulation of any undeclared dividends. The terms of the Series L Preferred Stock are more fully described in the Certificate of Amendment, a copy of which is attached hereto as Exhibit 3.1 and is incorporated herein by reference. This summary does not purport to be complete and is qualified in its entirety by reference to the Certificate of Amendment. &#160; ITEM&#8201;5.03. AMENDMENTS TO ARTICLES OF INCORPORATION OR BYLAWS; CHANGE IN FISCAL YEAR. On July&#160;17, 2026, M&T filed with the New York State Department of State the Certificate of Amendment for the purpose of fixing the designations, preferences, limitations and relative rights of the Series L Preferred Stock. The Certificate of Amendment became effective immediately upon filing, and the public offering of the Depositary Shares representing interests in the Series L Preferred Stock was completed on July&#160;21, 2026 (as described below). Holders of the Series L Preferred Stock will be entitled to receive, when, as and if declared by M&T&#8217;s board of directors or any duly authorized committee of M&T&#8217;s board, out of assets legally available for payment, noncumulative cash dividends based on the stated amount of $10,000 per share of Series L Preferred Stock (equivalent to $25 per Depositary Share). If declared by M&T&#8217;s board of directors or any duly authorized committee of M&T&#8217;s board, M&T will pay dividends on the Series L Preferred Stock through the redemption date of the Series L Preferred Stock, if any, quarterly, in arrears, on March&#160;15, June&#160;15, September&#160;15 and December&#160;15 of each year, beginning on September&#160;15, 2026. Dividends on the Series L Preferred Stock will accrue from the original issue date at a rate equal to 6.625% per annum for each dividend period. &#160; Upon the payment of any dividends on the Series L Preferred Stock, holders of Depositary Shares will receive a related proportionate payment. Dividends on shares of the Series L Preferred Stock will not be cumulative. Dividends on the Series L Preferred Stock will not be declared, paid or set aside for payment to the extent such act would cause M&T to fail to comply with applicable laws and regulations, including applicable capital adequacy rules, or for which we have not received applicable regulatory approvals. The Series L Preferred Stock is perpetual and has no&#160;maturity date. M&T may redeem the Series L Preferred Stock, in whole or in part, from time to time, on any dividend payment date on or after September&#160;15, 2031, or, in whole but not in part, at any time within 90 days following a regulatory capital treatment event (subject to limitations described in the Certificate of Amendment), in each case at a redemption price equal to $10,000 per share (equivalent to $25 per Depositary Share), together with an amount equal to any dividends that have been declared but not paid prior to the redemption date. The Series L Preferred Stock has voting rights only with respect to the following: amending M&T&#8217;s certificate of incorporation to authorize or increase stock ranking senior to the Series L Preferred Stock; amending M&T&#8217;s certificate of incorporation to effect certain changes in terms of the Series L Preferred Stock; consummating certain share exchanges, reclassifications, mergers or consolidations of M&T; electing directors following certain dividend non-payments; and as otherwise required by applicable law. Holders of Depositary Shares must act through Computershare Inc. and Computershare Trust Company, N.A., jointly as the depositary for the Depositary Shares, to exercise any voting rights of the Series L Preferred Stock. The terms of the Series L Preferred Stock are more fully described in the Certificate of Amendment, a copy of which is attached hereto as Exhibit 3.1 and is incorporated herein by reference.
