## SUMMARY

**Fox Corporation**([FOXA](https://www.fool.com/quote/nasdaq/foxa/)+3.62%) reported record fiscal 2026 financial results driven by the broadcast of the FIFA Men's World Cup and growth in its digital segments. Management highlighted the successful launch year of the FOX One streaming service and the expansion of Tubi's user base to 110 million monthly active users. The company reported that its pending acquisition of Roku is on track to close in calendar 2027, aimed at enhancing its connected television footprint and advertising capabilities. Financial performance was further supported by a strong advertising upfront market and disciplined digital investment, while the company maintained its long-term sports rights relationship with the NFL through the 2029 season.

*   Executive Chair and Chief Executive Officer Murdoch noted that the World Cup allowed the company to "top all networks in live event sports consumption in fiscal '26," even against competitors carrying the Super Bowl and Olympics.
*   Management reported that 96% of Tubi viewing is video on demand, which Murdoch stated indicates viewers are "proactively choosing to watch that content in their living room or in their home" rather than passive streaming.
*   The company reported that the launch of FOX One saw "minimal cannibalization" of the traditional pay-TV business, with new subscribers appearing to be incremental to the ecosystem.
*   CEO Murdoch confirmed that negotiations for NFL rights extensions will occur closer to the 2030 season, noting the relationship remains "incredibly positive."
*   Murdoch reported that Tubi now generates "tenfold" the revenue compared to when it was first acquired, reflecting a strategic shift in digital advertising scale.
*   CFO Tomsic stated that the company's buyback program is expected to "continue unabated through the pendency of the [Roku] transaction and beyond" due to balance sheet flexibility.
*   Management indicated that the upcoming midterm election cycle is expected to exceed the $260 million revenue record set during the previous midterm period.

## INDUSTRY GLOSSARY

*   **AVOD**: Advertising-based Video on Demand, a streaming service model where content is free to users and supported by advertisements.
*   **FOX One**: The company's direct-to-consumer streaming service launched in fiscal 2026.
*   **Midterm Election Cycle**: The period surrounding U.S. elections that occur two years after a presidential election, often characterized by high political advertising spend.
*   **MVPD**: Multichannel Video Programming Distributor, a service provider that delivers multiple television channels, such as cable or satellite companies.
*   **Retransmission Fees**: Payments made by cable and satellite providers to local broadcast stations for the right to carry their signal.
*   **Tubi**: A free, ad-supported streaming service owned by Fox Corporation.
*   **Upfronts**: An annual period where television networks sell the majority of their advertising inventory for the upcoming broadcast season.
