gross profit before depreciation and amortization at Trail increased to $203 million compared with $42 million in the same period last
year despite the planned shutdown of the lead circuit during the quarter.

at Trail. The initiative has the potential to expand production of germanium and antimony and add new gallium capacity. It also
remains subject to certain conditions as well as evaluation under our capital allocation framework.
