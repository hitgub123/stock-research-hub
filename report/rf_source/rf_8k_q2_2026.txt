Regions Financial Corporation (“Regions” or the “Company”) executives will make various presentations regarding, among other things, the Company’s operations and performance, to institutional investors at various meetings and events during the months of August and September 2026.

A copy of the materials to be used at these various meetings and events (the “Presentation Materials”) is being furnished as Exhibit 99.1 to this report, substantially in the form intended to be used.
